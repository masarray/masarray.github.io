# Community Code of Conduct

The MasArray Software Portal welcomes constructive participation from engineers, developers, creators, students, testers, and users.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and professional.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and claims rather than people.
- Explain technical disagreements with reproducible information where possible.
- Respect safety, confidentiality, intellectual property, and project boundaries.
- Help newcomers understand the context without ridicule or gatekeeping.
- Disclose relevant limitations and avoid presenting assumptions as verified facts.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, deliberate misinformation, publication of confidential material, and attempts to bypass safety or security boundaries are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior harms the community or project. Serious security or legal concerns may be escalated through the appropriate private channel.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the GitHub profile or an appropriate private security report. Do not expose sensitive personal information in a public issue.
